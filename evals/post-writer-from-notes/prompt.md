Write me a LinkedIn post from these notes. I'm a fractional CFO for e-commerce brands doing $2M-$20M a year, and I want founders of those brands to read it.

Notes: client is a skincare brand (can't name them). They thought they were profitable. Ads looked great, ROAS 3.4. But after returns (18% return rate), payment fees and shipping subsidies, they lost $4.10 on every first order. We fixed it by raising the free shipping threshold from $35 to $60 and cutting 2 SKUs that made up 40% of returns. Contribution margin on first order went from -$4.10 to +$6.30 in 7 weeks.

Here's how I usually write: "Most founders I talk to know their ROAS to the decimal. Almost none know what they keep after a return. That gap is where the money goes."
