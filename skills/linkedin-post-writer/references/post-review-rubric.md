# Post Review Rubric

Score each criterion 0–2. Be decisive: 0 is missing or harmful, 1 is present but
weak, 2 does its job.

| Criterion | 0 | 1 | 2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hook (first two lines) | A banned opening, or it only announces the topic | Clear, but could open any post on the topic | Specific, true to the body, and gives the intended reader a reason to open "see more" |
| One idea | Three or more points compete | One main idea with a detour | One idea, and every line serves it |
| Specifics | Adjectives only | One concrete detail | Numbers, names, or moments from real work carry the post (or marked placeholders waiting for them) |
| Reader fit | Written for peers or for everyone | A buyer could care | Names the reader's situation in words they would use |
| Voice | Banned phrases, em dashes, corporate gloss | Mostly natural | Sounds like the user's samples and passes the read-aloud test |
| Shape | A wall of text, or one-line drama | Readable | Paragraphs of 1–3 sentences with varied length; a text post near 900–1,300 characters, or shorter when the idea is complete |
| Ending | Bait, or a moral nobody asked for | Fine but forgettable | Stops when the idea stops: a takeaway, a real question, or a clear next step |
| Platform hygiene | Link in the body of a company page post, tag spam, hashtag stuffing | A minor slip | Links in the first comment, format matches the job, nothing spammy |

**Total: 16.**

## Verdict

- **13–16: Publish.** Mention any 1s, change nothing else.
- **9–12: Fix first.** Give the top fixes and an edited version.
- **0–8: Rewrite.** Go back to the idea and the specifics; editing will not save it.

## Hard fails

Whatever the score, the verdict cannot be Publish while any of these stands:

- A number, client, or result the user did not supply, or a statistic about the world
  with no source.
- Engagement bait: "Agree?", "comment YES", tag-a-friend, or any request for
  reactions.
- A banned phrase or banned opening from `voice-rules.md`.

Fix the hard fail in the edited version, or ask for what is missing.
