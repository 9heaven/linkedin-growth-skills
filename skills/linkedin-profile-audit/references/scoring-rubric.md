# Profile Scoring Rubric

Score each section 0–5. Be decisive: a 2 is a 2. Averaging everything to 3
produces an audit nobody acts on.

Anchors: **0** absent · **1** present but harmful · **2** generic ·
**3** functional · **4** strong · **5** best-in-niche.

## Headline (weight 5)

| Score | Condition |
|---|---|
| 0–1 | "Founder at X" only, or empty, or keyword soup |
| 2 | Title + company + a vague descriptor |
| 3 | Names an audience or an outcome, but not both |
| 4 | Audience + outcome, front-loaded in the first 60 chars |
| 5 | Audience + outcome + a differentiator or proof point, and it sounds human |

Check: does the first 60 characters stand alone? That is all that renders in
search results, comments, and notifications.

## About (weight 5)

Score the first two lines separately from the rest, because that is all a
visitor sees before "see more".

| Score | Condition |
|---|---|
| 0–1 | Empty, or a third-person bio, or a resume summary |
| 2 | Starts with "I have N years of experience" |
| 3 | Explains what they do, but buries it below line 5 |
| 4 | Hook in line 1–2, clear positioning, some proof |
| 5 | Hook + agitate + positioning + real proof + who-it's-not-for + one CTA |

## Featured (weight 4)

| Score | Condition |
|---|---|
| 0 | Section not enabled |
| 2 | Random posts, no thumbnails, no order |
| 3 | Relevant items but no direct conversion path |
| 4 | Booking or offer link first, custom thumbnails, benefit-led titles |
| 5 | The above plus a lead magnet and a case study, all 4 slots working |

## Banner (weight 3)

0 = LinkedIn default. 2 = a stock image or a logo alone. 3 = readable text.
4 = offer or proof, legible on mobile. 5 = offer + proof + a visual that is
unmistakably theirs, with the bottom-left kept clear for the profile photo.

## Experience, current role (weight 3)

0–1 = blank. 2 = duty list. 3 = describes the company. 4 = describes who the
company serves and the outcome. 5 = that, plus 3–4 proof bullets with numbers.

## Photo (weight 2)

0 = none or a logo. 2 = distant, dim, or a group crop. 3 = clear but stiff.
4 = face fills the frame, eye contact, good light. 5 = that, plus a background
and expression that fits the positioning.

## Activity (weight 5)

| Score | Condition |
|---|---|
| 0 | No posts ever |
| 1 | Last post over 6 months ago |
| 2 | Only reshares and congratulation comments |
| 3 | 1–3 original posts a month |
| 4 | Weekly original posts, some comments |
| 5 | 3+ posts a week with real comment threads |

Activity at 0–2 caps the value of every other fix. Say so.

## Skills and recommendations (weight 2)

0 = no recommendations, generic skills. 3 = top 3 skills match the positioning,
2+ recommendations. 5 = 5+ recommendations naming specific results, skills
ordered deliberately.

## Completeness (weight 1)

Custom URL set, location, contact info, website link, creator mode topics.
Binary items, all cheap. Score by fraction completed.

## Computing the verdict

Weighted score out of 100. Bands:

- **Under 40** — profile is losing people. Full rewrite.
- **40–60** — functional resume, not a conversion asset. Fix headline, About,
  Featured in that order.
- **60–80** — good asset. The bottleneck is traffic, not copy.
- **Over 80** — do not touch the profile. Work on content and offer.

Always report the weighted total *and* the three lowest weighted scores, because
those are the work.
