# LinkedIn Platform Reality

Hard numbers the skills rely on. **Verify quarterly** — LinkedIn changes distribution
without announcement, and a stale number in an audit destroys trust faster than no
number. Last verified: **September 2026**.

## Feed allocation and page reach

| Fact | Figure | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| Personal profiles' share of feed | ~65% | Where reach actually lives |
| Company pages' share of feed | ~1–2% | The page is not a reach engine |
| Page organic reach change, 2024 → 2026 | down 60–66% | Not your fault if page numbers collapsed |
| Personal vs page reach, identical content | personal ~5.6x higher (some analyses cite 561% more) | Post from the founder, reshare to the page |
| Initial page-post distribution | ~2–5% of followers | The first hour decides whether it goes further |
| Employee share rate vs page share rate | ~14.6% vs ~1.7% | Advocacy is the page's real growth lever |

Sources: AuthoredUp / Entrepreneur platform analyses, HubSpot marketing statistics,
2026 algorithm reporting. Figures vary by study — present them as ranges, never as
precise guarantees.

## Ranking signals

- **Dwell time** (how long people actually read) and **early meaningful comments**
  drive distribution. Likes barely move it.
- **Golden hour:** the first 60–90 minutes decide reach. Replies from the author in
  that window compound it.
- **Engagement bait is down-ranked.** "Agree? 👇" and tag-a-friend actively hurt.
- **Long shelf life:** suggested feeds resurface good posts for days or weeks. Judge a
  post at 48 hours minimum, saves at 7 days.

## Format effects

| Format | Effect |
|---|---|
| External link in post body | suppresses reach (commonly cited ~60% penalty). Put links in the first comment |
| Text-only company page post | ~0.42x reach vs posts with media |
| Document carousel (PDF) | highest save rates |
| Native video with captions | strong 2026 push from the platform |
| Questions and frameworks | large lift in views and saves |

## Account limits (personal profiles)

| Limit | Figure |
|---|---|
| Connection requests | ~100 invites/week; ~400/month non-premium |
| Total interactions/day | ~120 ceiling; stay well below |
| Safe daily connection requests | 10–20 |
| Safe daily DMs | 15–20 |
| Pending invites | keep under 500 |

Interactions counted include DMs, invites, InMails, profile views, likes, comments,
endorsements, and event/group joins.

## Account health thresholds

| Indicator | Healthy | Warning | Danger |
|---|---|---|---|
| Connection acceptance rate | >30% | 15–30% | <15% |
| Message response rate | >10% | 5–10% | <5% |
| Profile views trend | stable/rising | declining | blocked |

## Warm-up protocol (new, dormant, or restricted accounts)

An account that has been quiet for months should not jump straight to full activity.

- **Days 1–10:** max 60 interactions/day. Manual only. Views, likes, genuine comments.
- **Days 11–20:** up to 80/day. Begin 5–10 connection requests/day.
- **Days 21+:** approach normal limits gradually. Watch the acceptance rate.

Red flags that trigger restrictions: sudden activity spikes, identical messages,
activity concentrated outside business hours, and any automation on a cold account.

## Features that no longer exist

- **Creator mode toggle** — removed March 2024. Its features (newsletter, Follow
  button, analytics) are now available by default on eligible profiles. Do not ask a
  user to "turn on creator mode".
- **Profile hashtags / "Talks about" topics** — removed February 2024.

## Still live and underused

Newsletters (push notification + email to subscribers), native video, Showcase pages,
Page Events, "Notify Employees" on page posts, the Services section on personal
profiles, and the Products/Services tab on pages.

## Benchmarks, stated honestly

Engagement rate = (reactions + comments + shares) / impressions x 100.
For small B2B accounts, 3–5% is healthy; 1–2% is below target. Personal profiles
typically run ~4.7% against pages at ~1–2%.

Always compare a user to their own numbers from last month before comparing them to
any benchmark here.
