# LinkedIn Platform Reality

The figures these skills rely on, each with its source and how much weight it can
bear. LinkedIn changes distribution without notice, and a stale or overstated
number in an audit costs more trust than no number at all.

**Last verified: November 2026.** Re-check every quarter. Drop or downgrade any row
whose source is more than a year old or cannot be traced.

## How to cite a figure

| Grade | Meaning | How to say it |
|---|---|---|
| **LinkedIn** | LinkedIn published or stated it | State it plainly |
| **Study** | A third party measured it on a stated dataset | "One study found...", give the range |
| **Reported** | Widely repeated by practitioners or tool vendors; no primary data found | A working rule, never a fact. No false precision |
| **House rule** | This repo's own operating guideline | Present as advice, not as a platform fact |

Always give ranges or approximations. Never promise an outcome from any number here.

Old, and often misquoted as "personal vs page for identical content" |
| Employee networks vs page followers | employees have about 10x as many connections as the company has followers | LinkedIn | [LinkedIn Elevate infographic](https://business.linkedin.com/content/dam/me/business/en-us/elevate/Resources/pdf/elevate-infographic.pdf) |

**What to tell the user:** people reach several times further than the page (the
studies above range from about 2.75x to 6.6x). The page is a credibility asset;
reach comes from the founder and the team.

## Format effects

| Format | Effect | Grade | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Text-only company page post | 0.42x reach multiplier, the weakest format for pages | Study | [AuthoredUp 2025 algorithm data](https://authoredup.com/blog/linkedin-algorithm) |
| External link in the post body | LinkedIn has never confirmed a link penalty. Studies still measure lower reach (Ordinal: about 26.5% median deficit), concentrated on company pages; personal profiles show almost none | Study | [Ordinal link study](https://www.tryordinal.com/blog/linkedin-link-penalty-study) |
| Polls | median reach down about 35% year over year. LinkedIn said there were "too many polls in the feed" and shows fewer from people members don't know | Study + LinkedIn | [AuthoredUp](https://authoredup.com/blog/linkedin-algorithm), [Social Media Today, May 2022](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/linkedin-updates-feed-algorithm-to-downrank-engagement-baiting-posts-and-po/623313/) |
| Video | median reach down nearly 30% year over year | Study | [AuthoredUp](https://authoredup.com/blog/linkedin-algorithm) |
| Documents (PDF carousels) and images | median reach down about 8% and 10%, the smallest drops measured | Study | [AuthoredUp](https://authoredup.com/blog/linkedin-algorithm) |

What follows from this: on company pages always attach media and put links in the
first comment. On personal profiles a link costs little, but the first comment is
still the safe default when reach matters. Use video for trust, not as a reach
hack, and polls for research, not reach.

## Account limits (personal profiles)

LinkedIn does not publish exact limits. Every number here is **Reported** (by
outreach-tool vendors and practitioners) unless marked otherwise. Stay well under
all of them.

| Limit | Figure |
|---|---|
| Connection invitations | about 100 a week, on a rolling 7-day window |
| Safe invitation pace (house rule) | 10–15 a weekday. 20 a day spends the whole weekly budget in five days |
| Total actions a day | stay well under about 120: invitations, DMs, InMails, profile views, likes, comments, endorsements, and group or event joins all count |
| DMs a day | 15–20 |
| Pending invitations | keep under about 500; withdraw stale ones |

## Account health thresholds (house rule)

| Indicator | Healthy | Warning | Stop and fix targeting |
|---|---|---|---|
| Connection acceptance rate | above 30% | 15–30% | below 15% |
| Reply rate to first messages | above 10% | 5–10% | below 5% |
| Profile views trend | stable or rising | falling | a restriction notice |

## Warm-up protocol (Reported)

For new, dormant, or recently restricted accounts. Manual activity only.

- **Days 1–10:** up to 60 actions a day. Views, likes, genuine comments. No invitations.
- **Days 11–20:** up to 80 a day, including 5–10 invitations a day.
- **Day 21 on:** approach the normal pace gradually. Watch the acceptance rate.

Restriction triggers: sudden spikes, identical messages, activity concentrated
outside business hours, and any automation on a cold account.

Never ask a user to "turn on creator mode" or add profile hashtags. Follow can still
be set as the primary profile button in the Followers visibility settings.

## Live and underused

Newsletters (subscribers get a notification and an email), the Services section on
personal profiles, the Products tab and Showcase pages on company pages, page Events,
and the option to notify employees about a page post where the page offers it.

## Engagement benchmarks (house rule)

```
Engagement rate = (reactions + comments + reposts) / impressions x 100
```

Use the median of the last 5 posts, so one outlier does not decide the verdict.

Orientation bands for accounts under about 10K followers. These are not a published
LinkedIn benchmark:

| Engagement rate | Verdict |
|---|---|
| below 1% | weak |
| 1% to under 3% | below target |
| 3% to 5% | healthy |
| above 5% | strong |

Larger accounts usually run lower, and company pages usually run below personal
profiles. Other orientation ranges for small accounts:

- Profile views per 1,000 impressions: 5–15 when the hook and the identity align.
- Inbound DMs: 1–5 a week at 3 posts a week with a clear offer.
- Follower growth: 100–400 a month at a steady 3–5 posts a week.

Always compare a user with their own numbers from last month before comparing them
with anything here.
